New app, Lakitu, now available on the UMKL Discord Server

Lakitu is a new custom app for Discord that stores a user's time trial records across all games in the Mario Kart series.
Lakitu can store time trial records for any Mario Kart game in the series

The UMKL development team has created a new app, Lakitu, for storing time trial records across all games in the Mario Kart series.

The Lakitu app has been in development for several months following a request from members of the UMKL board regarding the potential of bringing a time trial based competition to the UMKL. This app is a stepping stone towards that goal, with features for time trial competitions on the horizon.

The app, currently in an early-access state, is available on the UMKL Discord server. Users can register with the app, and submit their time trial records to it across every Mario Kart game. Users have the choice between submitting each of their lap times, or submitting the total time of the record to speed up the process. Users can also choose their character and vehicle combinations to provide more context for their record.

Lakitu allows users to set records across all popular categories in each game, such as shroomless or 200cc records. Users can view leaderboards for each category on each course, and can compare their times against all other submitted records for the chosen category. The app also integrates with the community made Mario Kart standards sheets, categorising records into tiers based on how close they are to the corresponding world record. As suitable standards sheets are not yet available for every category in every Mario Kart game, some standards were created by the development team to ensure every record can be categorised.Users can also use the app to track their total time across all courses in a given Mario Kart game. This requires the user to submit a record for every course.

The app has the ability to host time-limited competitions. These would involve a course being declared, on which users have a limited amount of time to set time trial records on. The user with the fastest time at the end of the competition will be declared the winner. The competitions have not been tested at scale yet as the feature is still in development. The first tests of this feature are likely to take place on the UMKL Discord server in the near future. This feature will not affect the normal operation of the UMKL, which will remain a team-based racing series when Season 3 begins later in the year.

The design of the app allows it to be added to any Discord server. However, this feature is restricted at the moment as the app is still in early access. Time trial records will be unique to each server that the app is added to, and server owners will be able to configure the various settings of the app to suit their needs.

The development of Lakitu is expected to continue alongside Cheep Cheep, the UMKL's master app that manages several aspects of the league. The UMKL development team asks users to report any issues they find with the app as this will allow bugs to be fixed before the app leaves its early access state.

Directions for joining the UMKL Discord server are available in the social links on the website.
