New Teams

Now here are a few words from some of the new teams joining the UMKL for Season 3.

Oxford

There are more than 26,000 students at Oxford, including 12,460 undergraduates and 13,755 postgraduates.

Oxford formed a team but due to the limit of 12 teams being reached for Season 2, their team have been waiting for Season 3 to participate. Oxford held a test match with Staffs in November 2025, so they have some experience with 6v6 matches in Mario Kart 8 Deluxe.

We created our team in November of 2026
We are going to win the whole thing because we have heart, we have soul, and we're not all just gonna use Yoshi in the teddy buggy - Sans-san (board member from Oxford)

Leeds

Notable alumni from the University of Leeds include former prime minister of the United Kingdom Keir Starmer.

Representatives from Leeds enquired about joining the next season of the UMKL whilst Season 2 was ongoing, similarly to Oxford. They played a test match with Staffs in November 2025, scoring 554 - 430, which is a good result considering the that Staffs is a experienced team.

Bangor

The first team from a Welsh university to join the UMKL!

After talks with representatives, Bangor, also in November 2025, created their team, preparing for Season 3 of the UMKL!

"I'd personally wanted a Mario Kart league for ages, constantly begging NSE to add it to their roster of games. It wasn't until October 2025 where I randomly stumbled on a results post on Birmingham VS Liverpool where I learned of UMKL. I immediately looked through the website and joined the Discord to get more details. I was shocked to learn of a lack of Welsh teams, and saw an opportunity to represent Wales and Welsh universities. I am extremely excited for season 3, and cannot wait for our first game!" - Buckets (board member for Bangor)

Bath

The university pays a peppercorn a year to the city for rent of a 140-acre (57-hectare) parcel of land.

Bath created their team in August 2026, getting ready for Season 3 after an informal test match with Staffs.

Herts

The University of Hertfordshire is located 30km North of London.

The newly created Herts team primarily consists of members of the University of Hertfordshire's Nintendo Society, which plays a large variety of Nintendo games with a specific focus on the Mario Kart series. They have hosted several tournaments on Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, so have lots of racing experience.

Plymouth

Plymouth is home to the oldest operating distillery in England!

Plymouth created their team in September 2026, and will be joining playing in Season 3. Talks with Plymouth have actually been ongoing since October 2025!

Lincoln

Lincoln Cathedral was the world’s tallest building for over two centuries!

Lincoln created their team in September 2026 as well.

Thank You For Playing

The inclusion of even more teams represents the UMKL's effort to expand to all major areas of the UK. Thanks to everyone's efforts for keeping the UMKL fun and engaging!
