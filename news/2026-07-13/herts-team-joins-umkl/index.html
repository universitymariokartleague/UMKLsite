New team 'Herts' joins the UMKL to represent the University of Hertfordshire

Herts is the first new team to join the UMKL since the conclusion of Season 2, representing the University of Hertfordshire.
Herts' team emblem features a hart (a mature stag or deer), a symbol of Hertfordshire

Students from the University of Hertfordshire have formed a new team, Herts, which has now joined the UMKL. They are hoping to enter into the upcoming third season of the league.

The University of Hertfordshire has officially entered the UMKL with the formation of its brand new team, Herts. Herts is the first new team to join the league since the conclusion of Season 2, which could potentially lead to more new teams joining the UMKL in the near future.

The team primarily consists of members of the University of Hertfordshire's Nintendo Society, which was launched in January 2019. This society, while playing a large variety of Nintendo games, has a substantial focus on the Mario Kart series, having hosted several tournaments on Mario Kart 8 Deluxe throughout its lifetime. The Nintendo Society agreed to set up a team for the University of Hertfordshire after being contacted by the UMKL's growth team a few weeks ago.

The University of Hertfordshire is located approximately 30 kilometres north of London, an area which currently has no active UMKL teams. The creation of the Herts team represents progress in the UMKL's effort to expand to all major areas of the United Kingdom.

The newly created Herts team, along with several students from King's College London, played a team-based Mario Kart 8 Deluxe match against Staffs' UMKL team. This match, while not officially endorsed by the UMKL, is an early indication of the performance level of the Herts team, as they managed a score of 412-572 against Staffs. Considering that Staffs is one of the UMKL's best performing teams, this result is promising for the potential of the Herts team. However, the true performance of the Herts team alone cannot accurately be assessed from this match due to the team up with King's College London.

The University of Hertfordshire is located in Hatfield, Hertfordshire

The arrival of Herts corresponds with the build up to Season 3 of the UMKL, which Herts is likely to join. They will need to assess whether they will have the minimum number of members required to play in matches once the University of Hertfordshire's term begins in September. Before being able to officially join Season 3 of the league, Herts will need to complete a UMKL-endorsed test match to ensure they meet the specific requirements needed to compete in the season.

Season 3 is expected to be the most competitive season of the league yet, and Herts’ entry adds to the build up to the new season. Their performance potential is currently unknown, which makes their opening match of the season hard to predict. Judging by the results of their informal match against Staffs, their performance bears resemblance to teams like Glasgow and Sheffield, placing them in the mid-field of the UMKL teams currently. However, this is speculation, and proper judgements cannot be made until they complete their first official test match.

With more universities showing interest, Herts may be the first of several new entrants in the coming seasons. Talks are currently being held with students from the University of Bath, the University of Manchester, the University of Hull, and more.

Season 3 is expected to begin in October 2026, with an official date to be confirmed in the near future.
