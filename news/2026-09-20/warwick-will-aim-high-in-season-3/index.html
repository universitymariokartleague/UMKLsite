Warwick Will Aim High In Season 3!

An interview with Warwick ahead of the upcoming season!
Warwick joined UMKL in Season 2, achieving a respectable 5th place finish!

Warwick will aim high in Season 3!

Ahead of the largest student Mario Kart tournament in British History, I am sitting down with the teams fighting for the coveted title of the Best Mario Kart University. I had the pleasure of interviewing three competitors from Warwick to recap on how their debut season went, and to poke their heads about the upcoming competition.

I had the pleasure of speaking to Miku, a team captain from Warwick, alongside Jyrakeck and Sma, who both put in impressive performances last season. 

I firstly asked the group about "how they found Season 2" and "if they did better than they thought they would", as pulling 5th in their first competitive outing was no easy feat. Jyrakeck mentioned that "5th was better than they thought they would have ever expected" and "they were very happy with the result" while Sma cited that "they felt like they outperformed their test matches prior to Season 2". The theme was an overall enjoyment!

A screenshot from the Birmingham VS Warwick match

When asked if there was a "particular performance from the team or individual that was inspired" Miku quickly jumped to mention their "impressive performance against Birmingham in Round 4", which many before the season might have seen as an upset. The consensus was common between the team that this was their magnum opus, while Jyrakeck also cited that "teammate 'v1p3r' was a turning point for the team" and "helped them consolidate their decision making and confidence". During the discussion it was plainly evident that the team had really bonded during the last twelve months.

A screenshot from the Warwick VS Keele match

Warwick suffered their only loss last season to eventual champions York. With this in mind, I asked the three if they "took anything away from the match" and if it "impacted their morale in any way". Sma said that the match was "a bit of a reality check" while Jyrakeck and Miku both ensured me that "it was important to have a competitive spirit, but you also have to remember to have fun". Expanding further on this point, Miku said that Warwick has "some of the most whimsy in how they play" and called back to when "they picked Baby Park in the first round of a match" and "swapped to Larry" because it was funny.

Speaking on behalf of everyone at UMKL, we love those small moments of students having fun together; it's one of the principles we were founded on!

In response to the previous comments I simply had to ask if they planned on going into Season 3 with that "a little bit of a laugh mentality" or if they were going to "put on a warface". This managed to get a split reaction from the team, with Sma mentioning that the very question was "in dispute right now". Jyrakeck followed this up by saying that a "balance needed to be struck" and "everyone has their own idea of having fun". This was proceeded by a very intriguing comment from the player, who was that he thought "some things needed to be retired" (referring to taking risks and their track selection process).

We could be seeing more calculated moves from Warwick this season...

It wouldn't be a proper interview about Season 3 if I didn't ask who they "wanted to avoid" and who they "wanted to face". Surprisingly, both Jyrakeck and Sma have their eyes set on the same target: Edinburgh. Jyrakeck mentioned "knowing people from Edinburgh" and "it would be a cool brag" while Sma said he wanted to play Edinburgh to "see how ridiculously good JoshAR is". Miku decided that Birmingham would be a good opponent in Season 3, to "see how far they had come" and cited the "local proximity" was a contributing factor. The consensus was to avoid "York" because of, and I quote, "PTSD". York have only lost one league match since the UMKL was established in 2024, to Staffs in the final match of Season 2.

I rounded out the interview by asking the three "where they were aiming for Season 3" and if they "had anything to say to the UMKL", and I got a clear consensus. Warwick want to push for Top Three this Season, with Miku saying they have "gotten a lot stronger" and "haven't lost any key players" and Sma saying "they have improved a lot over summer with their internal events". I was left with an intimidating quote from Jyrakeck at the end of the discussion, citing that the "Chips Will Fall As They May", and if their punching performance from last season was anything to go off I wouldn't write them off challenging for the whole thing.

Warwick have the energy and mindset to make a dent this season, and I for one can't help but get excited with them, and wish them the best of luck in the coming months. Season 3 will kick off in October 2026, stay tuned to witness the best British Student esports has to offer.
