A Likeable Brighton Will Bring Good Vibes This Season

An interview with the Brighton team ahead of the upcoming season!
Brighton joined the UMKL in Season 2, but rounded out the table despite a big win over Keele!

A Likeable Brighton Will Bring Good Vibes This Season

Ahead of the largest student Mario Kart tournament in British History, I am sitting down with the teams fighting for the coveted title of the Best Mario Kart University. I had the pleasure of interviewing three competitors from Brighton to recap on how their debut season went, and to poke their heads about the upcoming competition.

I sat down with Dawn, Quill and Remi, the three captains of the Brighton team, to talk about the previous season and how it all unfolded for them, and where they were at for Season 3!

My first question for the group was centred around last season. I asked them "how they found it" and if they felt it was a "fair reflection into how they performed". The best response I got on this came from Dawn, who mentioned that "they could have finished second to last if they hadn't of got them points knocked off", but also said that "everyone else was more prepared than they were but they were happy to be in the top individual leaderboard". The latter energy was matched by Quill, who said "it was fair for them to come last" despite many games feeling "close". Brighton were the last team admitted to Season 2, so had far less time to prepare. 

The results for the Keele VS Brighton match

I was curious how Brighton managed to overcome Keele in their final match of Season 2, so my next question to the trio was if they "did something differently" and if it was a "change of mindset" that led them to their first win in UMKL. Dawn was quick to credit their fellow interviewee Remi on the change that their introduction brought, while Remi said that "Petey carried, no questions about it" and that "they front run while Petey bombarded Keele with shocks" . Quill mentioned that the team "hyped themselves up a lot more than normal" and they had "new people who knew what they were doing, so they were a lot more confident they could eek it out".

Remi even recalled a time where they hit their own teammate in comical fashion to make sure they also hit a Keele competitor, and that resulting in a 1-3 finish for Brighton. Dawn certainly had some questions about that!

This was our match!

I was naturally quite interested in Quill's comment on the team hyping themselves up before their match against Keele, so I asked them "how they hyped themselves up" and if there was "anything specific" that they did. The answers I received to this question told me just how unified their team is, and is great insight into how a UMKL team operates behind scenes. Quill said that they constantly mentioned that "This was our match" and they engaged in a "lot of fun posting" for vibes. Remi followed this up by saying that "they went into it with the attitude that it was the last game so they played with the intention of making sure it was a win". Dawn actually refused to elaborate on their own comment, just leaving it as a "new approach" and "the spirit of the South". Very mysterious...

Bringing things back on topic, I was wondering if they "had been practising" for Season 3, and if they were "prepared". Remi decided this was a humorous time to mention that they had "run out of Switch Online" so they had just been time trialling, but that some of their new players "have told them they are practising".  Dawn did also cite that the process of "passing the torch" had taken some time and focus away from practising, but ensured that me that the team is "ready for whatever is thrown their way in Season 3". Many of the Brighton team have been active in our Weekly Online Sessions, so I will certainly take their word for it!

As I did in the interview with Warwick, I had to ask the team who they "wanted to face" in Season 3 and who they "wanted to avoid". Quill mentioned as a blanket statement that "they would be happy if they did any better against any team than they did last season" while mentioning that they would "love to face Keele again sometime". Dawn actually threw Liverpool into the mix, calling it a "fun rematch". Remi just wants to "face anyone" as long as there are no CPUs involved.

My final question for the group was about their prospects for Season 3. I asked "where they were aiming", "who do you think will win Season 3" and "if there was anything they would like to say to the UMKL". My first response was from Remi, who said they are "aiming to win undoubtedly" but "there are some very strong teams so it'll be difficult", and I love the enthusiasm from this comment; why aim for anything but the top? Dawn was next to answer, saying that "after Season 2 they think York or Warwick have a huge shot, but for them personally they would be happy with middle of the table". My final answer of the question came from Quill who said "they want to win more than one game and aim to be more competitive with those better teams".

All three mentioned at the end of the interview that they loved being part of the UMKL. Quill said "thank you so much for putting a great league on and I hope to remain a part of it for as long as I can" and that "everyone has been so nice to them". Dawn said "thank you for having us, even if our joining was last minute and out of nowhere" and as a final statement, Remi decided to "wish everyone else good luck :p". It was evident from the start of the interview that Brighton were going to bring some serious energy to the new Season and, speaking personally here, even if they don't finish the highest they will leave a memorable impact on the league. 

Brighton are an interesting entry, with an almost infectiously positive attitude, and I think that many neutral spectators are going to be with them this season. I wish them all the best, and hope they make the climb up the table. Season 3 will kick off in October 2026, stay tuned to witness the best British Student esports has to offer.
