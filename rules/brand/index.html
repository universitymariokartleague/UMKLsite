Brand Guidelines

Guidelines for the use of intellectual property belonging to the UMKL

Text Guidelines

Please use our text guidelines to ensure consistency and readability across all UMKL content

  • Address the reader as "you" and the league as "we"
  • Refer the seasons like this: "Season 3", not "Season three" (same for rounds)
  • Write "UMKL" in all caps, always, (eg: the UMKL)
  • Write placings as "1st", "2nd", "3rd"
  • Please obtain permission before writing about a player, especially if using someones real name
  • Use they/them if you don't know someone's pronouns
  • Keep the tone inclusive and non-toxic when writing about players and results
  • Articles here generally use the Oxford comma

Social Media

Guidelines for posting our content online

Important
Promotion of the UMKL via society channels is allowed, but you must make clear that membership of that society isn't required to join the UMKL team. See the points below for specifics.
  • Please do not reupload full unedited matches from the UMKL channel onto separate channels. Clips and montages/highlights are OK and encouraged!
  • A disclaimer should be included in the description, which could go along the lines of:
    "This is a match/clip/content from the UMKL. Anyone who is a member of any of the universities playing in the UMKL can participate. You do not need to be a member of this society to join your university's UMKL team."
  • Please leave a link to https://umkl.co.uk and the Discord server when uploading content related to the UMKL.
  • Please do not obscure or edit information on our graphics. For example: cropping out the test match watermark in graphics uploaded to social media
