Overview

We bring together students from universities around the UK to compete in 6v6 matches in Mario Kart. These guidelines ensure all content involving the UMKL is consistent and aligns with our overall vision: a simple, intuitive brand identity that adapts to specific situations.

Our vision is to be modern, intuitive, bold and dynamic.

The UMKL is built by students, for students, and our brand should reflect that. It reaches potential players in a variety of places, so these guidelines apply across all of the UMKL: the Discord server, the website, YouTube, other social media, print, and video broadcasts. Consistent colours, shapes and typefaces convey the quality the UMKL strives for, and make everything feel like it came from the same place.

Writing

Keep UMKL content consistent and readable

  • Write "the UMKL" in capitals, not "the Umkl"
  • Write seasons and rounds with numbers: "Season 3, Round 2", not "Season three"
  • Write placings as "1st", "2nd", "3rd"
  • Address the reader as "you" and the league as "we"
  • Do not use generative AI in any graphics or media, please refer to the brand guidelines for design inspiration, or ask people for help
  • Get permission before writing about a player, especially if using their real name
  • Use they/them if you don't know someone's pronouns
  • Keep the tone inclusive and non-toxic when writing about players and results
  • Use the Oxford comma

Social Media

Guidelines for posting our content online

Important
Promotion of the UMKL via society channels is allowed, but you must make clear that membership of that society isn't required to join the UMKL team.
  • Clips, montages and highlights are encouraged, but please do not reupload full unedited matches from the UMKL channel
  • Do not crop or edit information on our graphics, like the test match watermark
  • Include links to umkl.co.uk and the Discord server when uploading UMKL content:

You can check out the UMKL website here: https://umkl.co.uk and be sure to join the Discord server here: https://discord.gg/jz3hKEmDss

  • Include a disclaimer in the description, along the lines of:

This clip is from a match in the UMKL. Anyone who is a member of any of the universities playing in the UMKL can participate. You do not need to be a member of this society to join your university's UMKL team.
